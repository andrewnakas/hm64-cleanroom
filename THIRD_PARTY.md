**Source offer for the GPL emulator core.** `n64wasm.js` / `n64wasm.wasm` are the unmodified build that
N64Wasm ships in its `dist/` folder at commit `bfac222f8a27287022844b47000328531834e9c1`. The complete
corresponding source (including the ParaLLEl N64 core under `code/`) is at
https://github.com/nbarkhina/N64Wasm/tree/bfac222f8a27287022844b47000328531834e9c1 .
If that link ever stops working, open an issue at https://github.com/andrewnakas/hm64-cleanroom and we will
provide the source.

**Game image.** `game.z64` is built from the decomp named above with every texture, font, picture and sound
sample regenerated by this project (https://github.com/andrewnakas/hm64-cleanroom). It is a fan project and is
not affiliated with or endorsed by the original publisher; game names are trademarks of their owners.
